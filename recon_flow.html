Why this shape

A figure is an amount plus a definition — as of when, which statuses count, gross or net, which taxes come off. Most disagreements are one of those, not a broken pipeline. So rather than asking why the two numbers differ, the replay recomputes ours under their rules until it lands on theirs. The combination that lands is the explanation, and it arrives with the query that proves it.

That search is cheap, so it runs as plain code over the causes we already know, and most claims never reach the agent. The agent starts where the known causes run out, and anything it finds has to hold at line level before it counts — with enough combinations, one will match by luck.

A person signs every reply, and every confirmed cause goes back into the history, where the ones that recur become rules the sweep applies. That is what makes the work shrink instead of repeat.

The dashed boxes are where I am guessing: how partners send their figures, and how disputes get raised and settled. Those change the shape; the rest stays.

It starts smaller than the picture. Write down what the team already knows, test each rule against periods already closed, and let the deterministic half grow out of real cases.
